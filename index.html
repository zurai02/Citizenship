Why the law keeps changing

As societies grow and modern technology advances, the law changes with them. There were, not long ago, no laws at all against cyber-exploitation — the harm didn't yet have a name the courts recognised. Civic rights and responsibilities move the same way: as a society grows, its laws update, and its politics shift to meet them.
